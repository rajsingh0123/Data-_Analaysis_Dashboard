# Data-_Analaysis_Dashboard
The Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of emergency department performance for a selected month and year. It is designed to help hospital administrators and healthcare staff monitor patient flow, service efficiency, and overall patient experience in a simple and visual manner.
Key Highlights of the Dashboard:

No. of Patients:
Displays the total number of patients who visited the emergency room during the selected period, giving a quick view of patient volume.

Average Wait Time:
Shows the average time patients waited before receiving medical attention, helping assess service efficiency.

Satisfaction Score:
Represents the overall patient satisfaction rating, indicating the quality of care and patient experience.

Patient Attendance Status:
A pie chart compares On-time versus Delayed patient attendance, helping identify service delays.

Gender-wise Analysis:
Visualizes the distribution of patients by male and female, supporting demographic analysis.

Admission Status:
Displays the number and percentage of admitted and not admitted patients, giving insight into emergency case severity.
Age Group Analysis:
A bar chart showing patient count across different age groups, helping understand which age ranges visit the emergency room most frequently.

Department Referral Analysis:
Shows how many patients were referred to different departments such as General Practice, Orthopedics, Cardiology, Neurology, etc., helping in resource planning.

Filters (Month & Year):
Interactive slicers allow users to analyze data dynamically by selecting specific months and years.
